Abstract

v4.0 grounds the FC loop—latent neural dynamics, conformal inversion, and holographic projection—within a neurobiological context. We simulate Wilson–Cowan population dynamics on hybrid small-world / scale-free graphs and compare synthetic against real EEG using five fractal metrics (Hurst H, spectral α, Δh, wPLI, statistical tests). A public intervention API enables perturb-and-measure experiments via directional transfer entropy and spatial propagation metrics.
